Measurable test: stayed% vs the 72.2% D.B. Cooper bar; comment rate vs Lucky Coin's 0.23% — an identity dilemma should beat the no-debate baseline, and comments should argue the tell, not the premise.
2. **Two-way-rule dilemma (The Visitor lesson, Glyph-ized).** Principle: post the monster's rule in frame zero, then give the protagonist a costly way to turn the rule back on the monster — the dilemma is whether using the loophole is worth what it costs, not whether the monster is scary. Evidence: The Visitor 220K likes / 350 comments on a 210K-follower account, with comments split between praise and plot-logic debate — the debate axis is the rule's fairness, which is exactly Glyph's debate engine. Uncertainty: one video, IG likes only, no retention data; a rule that is too clever reads as a puzzle, not a dilemma. Measurable test: stayed% vs the 72.2% D.B. Cooper bar; comment rate vs Lucky Coin's 0.23%, with comments coded for rule-fairness debate vs premise confusion.
3. **Law + anchor object + rule-breaker dilemma (Lullaby lesson, carried with a third instance).** Principle: open on a visible town law in frame zero, show the single anchor object that enforces it, then the character who breaks it — the dilemma is whether breaking the law is worth the cost, not the law itself.
