## Three recommended experiments (principles, never scripts)

1. **Noise-cost dilemma (Hollow Day 8, today only).** Principle: open on a hearer in frame zero with a visible sound cost — then force a choice where making noise saves someone but summons it, and staying silent saves yourself but costs them. The dilemma is whether the noise is worth the summoning, not whether the monster is scary. Evidence: Hollow Day 8 = Overseer (Warden), which hears from a great distance (Gamezebo Oct 29 entry); sound-as-cost is natively countable, which fits Glyph's debate engine. Uncertainty: chase mechanics are not choices — both sides must cost something real or it reads as a survival scene; IG/Y​T retention data for this mechanic is Unavailable. Measurable test: stayed% vs the 82% breakout bar; comment rate vs Lucky Coin's 0.24% — comments should argue noise vs silence, not the premise.
2. **Single-rule world with a visible cost (evergreen, from the sleep pop).** Principle: open on one rule stated in frame zero AND the cost of obeying or breaking it shown in the same frame — then force a choice where the rule itself is the trap (obeying it hurts someone, breaking it hurts you). The dilemma is which cost to take, not whether the rule is weird. Evidence: noirfilmsanimations' sleep rule at 122,648 likes on 20,509 followers (post-URL-verified); H5 single-rule comprehension shape. Uncertainty: likes only, no retention data; video not watched, premise caption-level; single account, single instance. Measurable test: stayed% vs the 82% breakout bar; comment rate vs Lucky Coin's 0.24%, with comments arguing which cost to take.
3. **Comparison-wrapper dilemma with a subject-carry test (capybluh lesson, refined).** Principle: frame the dilemma as a visible side-by-side comparison (two options, each with its catch shown in frame zero) — but test whether a single striking subject can carry the frame without the comparison, since capybluh's skeleton post hit the band solo. Evidence: capybluh comparisons hold ~1.3K likes while two bare-choice posts fell to 484–509; skeleton single-subject returned to 1,103. Uncertainty: IG likes only, single account, no retention data; Glyph's debate engine needs the comparison to carry a cost, not just a preference. Measurable test: stayed% vs the 82% breakout bar; comment rate vs Lucky Coin's 0.24%, with comments split between the two options (or arguing the single subject's cost) rather than asking what the choice is.
