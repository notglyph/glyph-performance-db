## Three recommended original creative experiments (principles + evidence + tests, never scripts)

1. **Single-rule power story WITH a pick attached (Time Freeze, fixed).**
   Principle: the rule draws the views, the pick draws the comments — Time
   Freeze proved the first half (23,476 views, 0.00% comment rate) and the
   missing half is the debate engine. Evidence: Time Freeze 23,476 views /
   503 likes / 1 comment; Sister Copies 19,737 views with the only real
   thread (17 comments) because its premise invites a "how would you do it"
   answer. Test: comment rate vs Time Freeze's 0.00% and Sister's 0.09%;
   stayed% vs Tornado's 82.1% (early) once Studio returns; a rate at or
   above ~1% with pick comments is the pass.
2. **Seat-count-closed personal-cost dilemma (Lifeboat, tightened) — carried,
   still unrun.** Principle: same personal stakes + visible vehicles/cost, but
   the seat/space limit is closed in-frame ("one seat each — no laps, no
   sharing, no second trip") so the lap-sitting move can't dissolve the binary.
   Evidence: Lifeboat 401K views, 78.9% stayed, lap-sitting dominated the 62
   sampled comments; Sister Copies' climb-up family (4/14) shows the
   solve-instead-of-choosing shape still recurs even outside dilemma titles.
   Test: stayed% vs Lifeboat's 78.9%; comment coding for lap/split loophole
   share vs Lifeboat's dominant share.
3. **Hidden-detail dilemma (H3's direct test, still unrun).** Principle: one
   findable detail planted in-frame (a number, a name patch, a background
   object) with the CTA pointing at it, giving comments a second engine
   besides the pick. Evidence: no Glyph upload has run a deliberate hidden
   detail; this week's four 1-comment sections show what happens without any
   comment engine at all (rates 0.00–0.04%). Test: comment rate vs the 1.61%
   Tornado / 0.48% Cliff baselines, coded for hunting comments vs pick
   comments; a rate at or above ~1% with hunting behavior is the first H3 pass.
