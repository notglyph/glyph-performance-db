## Three recommended original creative experiments (principles + evidence + tests, never scripts)

1. **Unavoidable-threat rescue dilemma (Tornado, tightened).** Principle: a
   loved one in danger from a threat whose lethality is arguable but whose
   path is visibly closed — no walk-around, no outrunning, no waiting it out —
   so the debate lands on the threat AND the choice instead of the escape.
   Evidence: Tornado 29,506 views, 82.1% early stayed, 476 comments, 1.61%
   rate, lethality debate verified; walk-around loophole family dominates its
   comments. Test: stayed% vs Tornado's 82.1% (early); comment coding for
   walk-around-loophole share vs Tornado's dominant share — a lower share with
   stayed% held is a pass.
2. **Seat-count-closed personal-cost dilemma (Lifeboat, tightened) — carried,
   still unrun.** Principle: same personal stakes + visible vehicles/cost, but
   the seat/space limit is closed in-frame ("one seat each — no laps, no
   sharing, no second trip") so the lap-sitting move can't dissolve the binary.
   Evidence: Lifeboat 401K views, 78.9% stayed, lap-sitting dominated the 62
   sampled comments; the batch's save-both recurrence (Frozen Lake 5/18,
   Earthquake 4/5) shows the shape is still unclosed. Test: stayed% vs
   Lifeboat's 78.9%; comment coding for lap/split loophole share vs Lifeboat's
   dominant share.
3. **Hidden-detail dilemma (H3's direct test, still unrun).** Principle: one
   findable detail planted in-frame (a number, a name patch, a background
   object) with the CTA pointing at it, giving comments a second engine
   besides the pick. Evidence: no Glyph upload has run a deliberate hidden
   detail; Tornado shows debate can fire without one (476 comments at 1.61%),
   so a planted detail + CTA could push the rate further or add a hunting
   thread alongside the debate. Test: comment rate vs the 1.61% Tornado /
   0.48% Cliff baselines, coded for hunting comments vs pick comments; a rate
   at or above ~1% with hunting behavior is the first H3 pass.
