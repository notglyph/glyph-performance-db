# Failed Patterns — what didn't work, and why it matters

Failures get the same rigor as breakouts. A technique common among winners *and* losers is not a winning technique.

## Glyph failures (Verified, owner analytics)

### Theft Parachute Dilemma.. (Oct 15) — hook failure
- 2.5K views vs 22K–67K typical; stayed finalized 58.1%; avg viewed 91.5%.
- Diagnosis (Inferred from the split): first-frame/hook failure, body fine. 42% swiped in the first seconds.
- Lesson: distribution flatlined within ~20h (+134 views in 12h) and never restarted — an early flatline is close to final.
- The video was later removed from the channel after its re-cut shipped.

### The D.B. Cooper Dilemma.. (Oct 17) — re-cut that didn't fix the swipe
- 16.5K views at ~61h vs 41K–97.4K typical; stayed 71.3%; avg viewed 101.4%.
- The re-cut changed the first 3 seconds; the split signature persisted and Studio still headlined "more viewers swiped away."
- Lesson: re-cutting the opening is not the same as making the dilemma visible in frame one. Also: reposting a re-cut carried **no distribution penalty** — the reupload instinct was validated even though the idea's ceiling looks real (~71% stayed across both versions).
- Open question: was the ceiling the premise (parachute dilemma, third outing of the shape) or the frame? Unverified — the next one-resource dilemma with a fully visible first frame is the test.
