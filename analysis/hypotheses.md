## Added Oct 20, 2026 — Operation 20/20 study (reports/first_20_video_study.md)

### H10 — Question-form hooks beat premise statements
- **Claim:** A hook that poses an unresolved question or violated expectation outperforms the same premise stated flat, within a creator's own catalog.
- **Evidence:** Study sample A/B split (8/10 vs 7/8). Glyph-side: untested.
- **Counterexamples:** Premise-statement world rules are H5's evidence base and won for these same creators on other days; craftee fatigue confound (18 pop-offs in 8 days before the typicals).
- **Sample size:** 18 videos (10 A / 8 B). **Independent creators:** 7. **Confidence: LOW–MODERATE** (observational, hook text only — no frame data).
- **Test:** Experiment 1 + a question-form restatement of a planned premise (see study Phase 5). **Status: UNTESTED for Glyph.**

### H11 — Reversal-bearing stories beat premise exploration
- **Claim:** Within a creator, stories with a mid/late reversal (enemy→ally, sinister→innocent, inverted goal) outperform premise-only explorations.
- **Evidence:** @craftee_films within-creator split in the study sample; A8/A9 reversals in Group A.
- **Counterexamples:** A2 math puzzle (no story, biggest multiple) — participation is a separate road; single-creator weight is heavy.
- **Sample size:** ~8 videos, 3 creators. **Confidence: LOW–MODERATE.** **Test:** Experiment 2. **Status: UNTESTED for Glyph.**

### H12 — The question must be visible, not just spoken
- **Claim:** A spoken question hook over a visually neutral opening frame does not stop the swipe; the dilemma/question must be readable from the visuals at ~0:00.
- **Evidence:** First verified frame-zero in the system (Mission 003): D.B. Cooper opens on a two-character cabin conversation — narration asks "Would you trust your best friend with your life?" but no parachute, danger, or choice is visible; stayed finalized 71.3% with avg viewed 101.4% (loss at the swipe, body held).
- **Counterexamples / gaps:** n=1, own channel; the same video was a re-cut of a previously capped premise (Theft Parachute 58.1%), so premise ceiling is a rival explanation. Oxygen Mask / Escape Pod openings have not yet been frame-verified for contrast.
- **Sample size:** 1 video, verified by direct playback + captions (Oct 20, 2026). **Confidence: LOW** (single case, but the first *verified* visual evidence in the register).
- **Test:** Mission 002 Experiment 1 (whole dilemma in frame one). **Status: UNTESTED.**

## H12 revision — Oct 20, 2026 (Mission 003 completed)

Competitor evidence landed (21 frame-verified videos): winners' openings were premise-readable 9/10 vs losers' 7/10 overall, but the split is creator-shaped — Xenost A 4/4 vs B 1/4 (including the sample's only two EMPTY frame zeros, both losers), while MiAnimation1's losers were 4/4 readable and lost on subject/pairing instead. H12 is therefore reframed: **frame zero must carry information** (a premise, contradiction, threat, or choice) — an empty or talking-only opening is the loser signature; a readable opening is necessary-looking but provably not sufficient. Confidence: LOW–MODERATE (up from LOW). D.B. Cooper remains the Glyph-side case matching the loser profile.
