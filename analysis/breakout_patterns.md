# Breakout Patterns

What separates videos that outperform their channel's baseline from those that don't. Every entry is a hypothesis with evidence, counterexamples, and confidence — see `hypotheses.md` for the register.

## Velocity clustering (hot-account tailwind)

- **Hypothesis:** Once a creator pops off twice in a short window, the algorithm keeps feeding the account, so their next posts — even ordinary coverage — distribute well above baseline.
- **Supporting:** nepshublox — 13+ pop-offs in ~2.5 weeks (Oct 2026), including straight coverage posts at ~8–10x; craftee_films — ~18 pop-offs in 8 days, with back-catalog compounding ("1,000,000 Steps" 106.8K → 304.7K likes in 24h); dylanstoriesyt — 3 pop-offs Oct 10–17, all still rising.
- **Counterexamples:** blooporobloxs had hits earlier in 2026 and has now been silent/flat for 25 days — heat decays; imcrellar's hits are streaky with flat posts between.
- **Sample size:** 3 creators with clear clusters (independent accounts, many videos).
- **Confounders:** Posting frequency rises when creators smell heat (more shots on goal); seasonal events (Halloween) inflate whole lanes at once.
- **Confidence:** Moderate.
- **Application to Glyph:** When a Glyph video starts outperforming, the next 48h is the highest-leverage posting window — have the follow-up ready before it's needed.
- **Next test:** Track whether Glyph's own post-breakout uploads outperform its baseline more than its ordinary uploads do.

## Novelty-drop speed coverage

- **Hypothesis:** Being first to cover a new game drop/popular update outperforms, regardless of production quality — the audience treats the account as a news desk.
- **Supporting:** nepshublox, 7 instances (mounts news, mutation showcases, Halloween fishing pair at 9.8x/7.9x on the same night, Oct 18–19).
- **Counterexamples:** None logged yet — but all instances are one creator, so this may be an account property, not a format property.
- **Sample size:** 7 videos, 1 creator.
- **Confounders:** The account's velocity cluster (above) may be doing the work.
- **Confidence:** Low–moderate.
- **Application to Glyph:** Weak direct fit (Glyph is dilemma fiction, not coverage) — but a dilemma built on a same-week game drop could borrow the speed tailwind.
- **Next test:** Watch for a second creator whose drop-coverage pops without a prior hot streak.
