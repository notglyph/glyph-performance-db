# Storytelling Patterns

## Escalation chains (cause-and-effect, BUT/THEREFORE)

- **Hypothesis:** Stories where each beat *causes* a worse next beat (mundane trigger → absurd, ever-bigger consequences) retain better than sequences of unrelated events.
- **Supporting:** nepshublox fan-police (182x), boxing (81x), parking-spot feud (26x), grandma (27.3x); craftee_films sleepwalking night-shift (16.7x), chess-accident (32.8x); fearless.gman impostor surge (152x). 8+ instances, 3 creators.
- **Counterexamples:** Not yet systematically logged — ordinary escalation-chain videos that went flat would weaken this; the tripwire now also logs normal/underperformers to check.
- **Confounders:** Both lead creators were in velocity clusters during several of these hits.
- **Confidence:** Moderate.
- **Application to Glyph:** The president-escalation brief (Oct 19) is built on this: each accident caused by the previous one, no disconnected beats.
- **Next test:** Compare a Glyph escalation-chain upload against a Glyph unrelated-events upload of similar topic/length.

## Single-rule worlds

- **Hypothesis:** One impossible rule, stated as fact in second person ("In this town, you get 1,000,000 steps"), gives instant premise comprehension and endless episode variants.
- **Supporting:** craftee_films — 6+ rule premises in ~36h (Oct 17–19), three tripling in 24h; Glyph's own "The Zero Gravity World" (81.6% stayed, near-miss) is the same shape.
- **Counterexamples:** craftee's "If You Move You Turn Invisible" (Oct 19) stayed below the pop-off line — the shape doesn't guarantee distribution.
- **Sample size:** 7+ videos, effectively 1 competitor creator + 1 Glyph video.
- **Confidence:** Moderate for premise comprehension; low for guaranteed performance.
- **Application to Glyph:** Already in the format lab — quantified rules beat abstract ones; the viewer should be the subject.

## Inverted goals

- **Hypothesis:** Win-by-losing premises invert the viewer's prediction and hold attention. craftee race (20.9x), nepshublox boxing (81x). 2 creators, confidence low–moderate. Untested by Glyph.

## Study addendum — Oct 20, 2026 (Operation 20/20)

Within-creator comparison (@craftee_films, both groups): breakouts carried an emotional arc (BEST FRIENDS) or an inverted goal (Finish LAST) — both generate BUT/THEREFORE reversals structurally — while same-week typicals were premise explorations without a logged reversal. Supports H4 and adds H11 (reversal-bearing > premise-only, same creator). Counterexample: the sample's biggest multiple (roblxify math puzzle, 182x) has no story at all — participation is a separate road.
